## Platform

web

## Users

The primary users are people in romantic relationships who want to create a personal digital gift for their partner. A typical user is a boyfriend or girlfriend preparing something for an anniversary, Valentine's Day, a birthday, or simply as an affectionate surprise without a special date.

The recipient experiences the finished gift as a digital scrapbook or diary, opening and exploring it page by page.

## Product Purpose

Scrapbook helps someone turn shared memories, messages, photos, music, and decorative elements into a personal digital gift that feels handmade.

Success means the creator can make something expressive and recognizably their own without needing professional editing skills, and the recipient experiences it as a thoughtful object rather than a generic web page.

## Positioning

Unlike generic romantic-gift sites, Scrapbook is specifically built around the metaphor and creative freedom of a physical scrapbook. It combines predefined themes that make starting easy with a flexible canvas where the creator can reposition and resize content, change colors, add and move photos, and drag decorative elements such as stickers onto the pages.

The result should feel like a real handmade scrapbook or diary that the recipient opens, not a fixed greeting-card template with replaced text.

## Operating Context

The creator begins with a predefined occasion or theme, then personalizes a page-based scrapbook in a visual editor. They can compose pages with text, photos, music, and decorative elements, preview the recipient's experience, publish it, and share it digitally by link or QR Code.

The product is intended to work well on mobile both for creating the gift and for opening it, while remaining a web product.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Predefined themes and templates provide a starting point.
- The editor is intended to support free positioning and resizing of elements, color adjustments, photo uploads and movement, and a library of draggable decorative elements such as stickers.
- The published gift is experienced as a sequence of scrapbook or diary pages.
- Music is a committed product capability.
- Editing after publication is a committed product capability.
- Link and QR Code sharing exist in the current product flow.
- The current checkout uses a development-only manual payment provider. Final payment method, launch pricing, plan limits, and gift lifetime remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

The current product name is Scrapbook.

The defining product metaphor is a real scrapbook or personal diary made by hand: creative, intimate, layered, and visibly assembled by the gift giver. Predefined themes must not reduce the creator's ability to make the result personal.

The customer-facing product is currently written in Brazilian Portuguese.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains working product flows for choosing an occasion and template, creating and editing a gift, managing media, previewing and reviewing it, checkout, publication, post-publication sharing, QR Code generation, and viewing the public gift.

## Product Principles

1. Make the gift feel authored, not generated: the recipient should recognize the creator's choices and effort.
2. Start quickly, personalize deeply: templates remove the blank-page barrier without becoming rigid containers.
3. Preserve the handmade scrapbook metaphor throughout creation and viewing.
4. Make creative manipulation direct: moving, resizing, recoloring, layering, and decorating should feel understandable and playful.
5. Never turn placeholder launch copy into a factual claim without evidence.
